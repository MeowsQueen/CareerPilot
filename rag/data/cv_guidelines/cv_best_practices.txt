Title: CV Best Practices for Students

Purpose:
This guide explains how university students should structure their CVs for internships and junior roles.

Recommended Sections:
- Contact Information
- Education
- Technical Skills
- Projects
- Internship or Work Experience
- Certifications
- Languages
- Extracurricular Activities

Best Practices:
- Keep the CV clear, concise, and easy to scan.
- Use bullet points instead of long paragraphs.
- Start bullet points with action verbs such as developed, designed, analyzed, implemented, or improved.
- Highlight measurable achievements when possible.
- Put the most relevant skills and projects near the top.
- Tailor the CV to the target role.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as expert, perfect, or guaranteed success.
- Do not include sensitive personal information such as gender, religion, marital status, or national ID.

For Computer Engineering Students:
- Emphasize programming languages, frameworks, databases, and tools.
- Include GitHub, portfolio, or project links if available.
- Describe projects with technologies used, problem solved, and outcome.
